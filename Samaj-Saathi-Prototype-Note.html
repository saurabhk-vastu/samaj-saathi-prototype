A smoother, more engaging journey for the same product

Prepared by Saurabh Kaushik

This clickable prototype keeps the existing Samaj Saathi features and flow intact. The work is focused on UI and UX, so the journey feels clearer, more interactive, and easier to move through.

About this prototype

I built this after a conversation where the next step was clear: create a Samaj Saathi prototype that improves how the app feels, without changing what the product does.

So the screens still do the same jobs. What changes is the presentation: calmer visuals, better hierarchy, cleaner spacing, and interactions that feel more inviting from the first tap to the last.

What stayed the same

Biodata creation, preferences, browsing matches, likes, messages, profile settings, help, language, and recharge are all still here.

The aim was not a new product. It was a more polished version of the same journey, so families can understand and explore the experience with less effort.

How to use the app

  1. Start the journey. Open the link above. Choose your language, enter any valid Indian mobile number, and enter OTP 1234.
    No real SMS is sent in this prototype
  2. Complete the biodata. Move through the onboarding screens as you would in the live app. Your selections carry forward, and you can go back without losing what you chose.
  3. Land on Home. Browse matches from major states, open Shortlist for profiles you like, and use the filter icon to refine preferences. The recharge banner stays visible and opens the ₹1 trial screen.
  4. Explore a match. Tap View profile to see details, then try Call, WhatsApp, Message, Like, Report, or Get help from the profile actions.
  5. Visit the other tabs. Use Likes, Messages, and Profile to see the rest of the journey. From Profile you can update details, change language, open help, and check account settings.

A gentle note while you explore

As you move through the prototype, notice how easy each step feels, how clearly the information sits on the screen, and whether the overall experience still feels like Samaj Saathi. Wherever a real payment, SMS, or backend would normally sit, this demo uses a simple stand-in so you can keep going without interruption. Feedback on anything that feels unclear or out of place is always welcome.
